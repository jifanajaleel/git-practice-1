RESUME Preparation:
--------------------

Are the CV & Resume the same?
Resume used for a specific job application. CV is more detailed
+---------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
|         		CV      	   		  |      		  Resume		       |
+---------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| More than 2 pages					  | Max 2 pages					       |
| Used for academic purposes				  | Used to apply for jobs			       |
+---------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+

*)Contents of a resume:
1. Name
2. Subtitle (eg: frontend developer)
3. Contact details
4. Experience
5. Projects (summarize and add project link too)
6. Skills (eg: html, js, version control, git)
7. Education (b-tech)
8. Certifications
9. Social media presence (eg: linkedIn, github)
10.Objective/summary

*)Use Keywords used in job description

*)Place the most impressive things about you at the Top of the Resume

*)Preferred location can be added

*)If you don't have much Experience, add your Projects instead:
1. What have you done(use action words like managed, initiated, collaborated etc along with Project name)?
2. How did you do that (technologies, softwares used)?
3. What's the impact?

*)Add LinkedIn profile, GitHub profile, Personal website

*)Add details in Reverse Chronological order - Last course / experience as first

*)Avoid mistakes

*)Keep it simple. Don't use many fonts/colors. Font size -> 10-12pt for normal text & 14-16pt for section header. Use PDF format. Don't have to put photo or DOB in resume

*)Tools/resources used to create a resume:
1. Microsoft word
2. https://flowcv.com/   (you'll have to create an account in it. Only 1 resume can be created. To create more, we should pay. But you can update on the same resume itself)

*)NAME you resume. eg:
Jifana Jaleel-Resume.pdf
Jifana Jaleel-Backend developer-Resume.pdf

*)Best time to apply for a job
1. Time - 6-10am
2. 1-4 days after the job post

check resume_example.png

*)Emailing a job application:
1. Subject: Applying for [job position] - your name
2. Body - Cover letter. It's a one-page document that you submit as part of your job application (along with CV or resume). Its purpose is to introduce you and briefly summarize your
professional background. Customize acc to job description (use chatgpt)
3. Signature (your name & contact)
