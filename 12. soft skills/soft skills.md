Analytical skill:
------------------

1. Write the application details in sentences

2. Identify **DATA** (Most of the nouns will be data).

eg: for an ecommerce app-> customers, products, product variants, cart, shipping address, payment modes, order delivery will be data

3. Identify **PROCESSES**/actions (Most of the verbs will be processes).

eg: login/sign in, searching for products, adding them into cart

4. Identify **SCREENS**. List all the screens \& then draw. 

eg: Home screen - This will have banner, offers.

Search screen - 

You can use draw.io to draw screens. Or else use pen \& paper

5. Sequence diagram:

eg: searching for product->adding to cart->shipping page->payment page->order gets confirmed->vendor ships->product gets delivered
